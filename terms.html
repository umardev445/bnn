Terms of use

KhataBanao is a lightweight, local-first ledger for recording customer credit and payments.

Using this tool

You are responsible for entering accurate customer data, recording payments correctly, securing your device, and obtaining permission where required by applicable law. This app does not process real payments, perform bank transfers, provide debt collection services or verify a customer's identity.

Records and backups

No automatic cloud backup is included. Data may be lost if your device is reset, browser storage is cleared, or the application is removed. Export backups regularly and store them securely.

No professional advice

This product is not a substitute for accounting, tax, financial or legal advice. Validate records before making business decisions.

Availability

The app is offered as-is, subject to applicable laws. Features in Android shells depend on the host app's native integrations. The publisher should keep these terms aligned with the actual released product.
