Help & getting started

Keep customer udhaar and payment history organised in a few easy steps.

1. Add a customer

Open Customers → Add customer, enter a customer name and optionally phone or notes, then Save.

2. Record udhaar

Open Transactions → New entry, choose the customer and Udhaar diya. Enter the amount, date and optional details. Save to increase their balance.

3. Record a payment

Add a new entry with Payment received to reduce the customer's outstanding balance. Payments larger than credit appear as an advance (negative balance).

4. Check a customer statement

Open Customers, select the customer, and use Print statement in a supported browser to print or save as PDF.

5. Protect your records

Go to Settings → Download backup for a JSON copy. Restore replaces the current local data, after confirmation. You can also Export CSV for spreadsheets.

Common questions

Does it work offline? After successfully opening the HTTPS site and caching files, many supported browsers can reopen it offline. Offline access in Android WebView depends on its setup. Your entries are local in the current browser profile.

Will my data sync between phones? No. You must manually export and restore a backup. Backup files are not encrypted.

Why won't a download work in my APK? Basic Android WebView does not automatically download Blob files or implement print; the native host needs integration. See ANDROID-NOTES.md in the project.
