KHATABANAO / INFORMATION

Privacy policy

KhataBanao is a simple customer ledger that stores records in the local browser or WebView on your device.

What you enter

The app can store customer names, phone numbers, notes, amounts, dates, payment/udhaar entries and display preferences. These entries may contain personal information about your customers. Use it only when you have a legitimate reason to record this information.

Where the data stays

The website version stores ledger data in your device's browser localStorage. It does not provide accounts, automatic cloud sync or a backend database. The developer does not receive the ledger entries through the app code. Hosting providers may still log ordinary website requests (such as IP address and browser information).

Third-party services

The provided website code does not include analytics, ad SDKs, payment processors, third-party scripts or remote fonts. The public website host has its own infrastructure and policies. Any native Android wrapper or later SDK additions must be disclosed separately.

Backup and deletion

You can export JSON and CSV files, which are saved wherever you choose. These files are not encrypted. Anyone who obtains them may read customer information. You can delete individual entries, customers without entries, or all local data through Settings. You can also clear this website's browser storage. We cannot remotely recover local records.

Data security

LocalStorage is convenient but is not an encrypted, password-protected vault. Protect your device and backup files. Do not use this tool for highly sensitive data without appropriate safeguards.

Contact

Before publishing, configure a real developer name and support email in assets/config.js and provide a monitored public contact channel. See the contact page.
